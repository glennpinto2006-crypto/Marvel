## Brand & Style

This design system delivers a high-impact, cinematic dark aesthetic rooted in blockbuster superhero iconography. Built for immersive digital experiences, high-tier entertainment platforms, and premiere fan hubs, the system evokes a commanding sense of power, prestige, drama, and adrenaline.

The aesthetic fuses **High-Contrast Cinematic Brutalism** with **Modern Sleek Minimalism**:
- **Massive, Assertive Impact:** Display elements use towering, razor-sharp condensed typography set exclusively in uppercase to project gravity and tension.
- **Deep Obsidian Immersion:** A disciplined 70% deep void base anchors the viewport, creating negative space reminiscent of an IMAX theater screen before the reel begins.
- **Controlled Kinetic Energy:** Vibrant crimson accents and prestige gold highlights are deployed strictly as focal detonators—guiding the gaze to primary actions, status shifts, and tier rankings without visual fatigue.
- **Atmospheric Glows & Tactile Finishes:** Restrained emissive drop shadows in crimson tint and hairline graphite borders give flat screens physical, architectural presence.

## Colors

The color architecture obeys a rigorous 70-20-8-2 distribution rule to maintain tension and prestige.

### Surface System (70%)
- **Canvas Base (`#080808`):** The absolute background void. Used for the root canvas and immersive backdrop sections.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#0D0D0D`):** Primary card bodies, navigation bars, and drawer containers.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#151515`):** Raised modules, nested panels, list containers, and active state panels.
- **Surface Layer 3 (`#202020`):** Modal dialogues, popovers, and elevated contextual menus.
- **Hairline Border (`#242424` / `#2A2A2A`):** Low-contrast architectural structural lines framing dark panels against darker canvas backgrounds.

### Typography & Content (20%)
- **Text Dominant (`#FFFFFF`):** Primary headings, impactful metric callouts, and key data anchors.
- **Text Secondary / Cool Silver (`#D0D0D0`):** Body narrative, list descriptions, and metadata.
- **Text Muted (`#8A8A8A`):** Captions, timestamps, disabled indicators, and structural label prefixes.

### Signature Marvel Crimson Accents (8%)
- **Crimson Core (`#E62429`):** Primary CTA buttons, key active indicator bars, and vital notifications.
- **Crimson Vivid Hover (`#FF3439`):** Interactive state illumination, active hover borders, and glow center-points.
- **Crimson Deep (`#8B1116`):** Tonal badge fills, pressed CTA foundations, and gradient edge-stops.

### Prestige Gold Highlights (2%)
- **Prestige Gold (`#F5C518`):** Reserved for tier badges, hero classifications, ratings, achievements, and VIP status accents. Never used for general buttons or continuous text.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy juxtaposes bold condensed display lettering against clinical, ultra-legible body prose:

- **Bebas Neue (Headlines):** Set exclusively in all-caps. Commands instant authority with dense vertical tension. Always apply optical letter tracking (+0.04em to +0.06em) to evoke theatrical marquee billing.
- **Manrope (Body & Content):** Selected for its geometric stability and neutral legibility against dark backdrops. It avoids eye strain during sustained reading.
- **Space Grotesk (Labels & Overlines):** Technical and futuristic. Applied strictly to small badges, field counters, categorizations, and tactical telemetry tags, always uppercase with wide letter tracking.

## Layout & Spacing

This design system uses a widescreen-oriented **12-column fluid grid system** with fixed outer constraints:

- **Desktop (1200px+):** 12 columns, 1.5rem (`24px`) gutters, and dynamic margins with a max content container width of `1440px`.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8 columns, 1.25rem (`20px`) gutters, and 2rem (`32px`) margins.
- **Mobile (Under 768px):** 4 columns, 1rem (`16px`) gutters, and 1.25rem (`20px`) side margins.

Content layouts favor wide cinematic ratios (16:9, 21:9 hero blocks, and 2:3 vertical character/poster ratios). Spacing adheres to an 8pt architectural rhythm, with generous outer component paddings giving cards and sections breathing room to heighten the epic, theatrical scale.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is established via subtle dark surface layering, razor-thin perimeter borders, and directional atmospheric red illumination rather than standard gray dropshadows:

- **Base Layer (Elevation 0):** Pure `#080808` canvas. Flat, unlit.
- **Tier 1 Container (Elevation 1):** `#0D0D0D` fill, bound by a crisp hairline border `1px solid #242424`. No box shadow in resting state.
- **Tier 2 Layer (Elevation 2):** `#151515` fill with `1px solid #2A2A2A` and an ambient perimeter shadow: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.8)`.
- **Cinematic Atmospheric Glow (Active / Focused / Hover):** Applied to interactive cards, primary CTAs, and featured hero components:
  - *Resting Glow:* `0 0 0 1px #2A2A2A, 0 4px 20px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.7)`
  - *Hover Glow:* `0 0 0 1px #E62429, 0 8px 30px rgba(230, 36, 41, 0.28), 0 2px 8px rgba(230, 36, 41, 0.4)`
- **Prestige State (Gold Accents):** Reserved for elite tier cards and achievements: `0 0 0 1px #F5C518, 0 8px 25px rgba(245, 197, 24, 0.22)`.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Soft Architectural (`1`)** geometry. 

- Standard components (buttons, input fields, cards, and modal panels) utilize a disciplined `0.25rem` (`4px`) corner radius.
- Large contextual hero panels and dialog viewports step up to `0.5rem` (`8px`) (`rounded-lg`).
- The silhouette intentionally avoids pill-shaped or bubbly curvature to preserve the sharp, athletic precision of armor plating, tech interfaces, and theatrical title plates.
- Badges and status pills remain slightly angular with `2px` to `4px` radii, maintaining military and comic-book precision.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action:** Solid Crimson `#E62429` fill with pure `#FFFFFF` typography (`label-caps`). Flat, no gradients. Hover transitions to `#FF3439` with an atmospheric crimson drop glow `0 0 20px rgba(230, 36, 41, 0.45)`. Transform scales subtly by `1.02` using a cubic-bezier easing (`cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`).
- **Secondary Action:** Pitch black fill (`#0D0D0D`) with a `1px solid #2A2A2A` border and `#D0D0D0` text. Hover transitions border color to `#E62429`, text to `#FFFFFF`, and applies a subtle crimson haze.
- **Prestige Button:** Used strictly for premier unlocks or collector actions. Pure Gold `#F5C518` fill with `#080808` bold uppercase text. Hover state brightens to `#FFD633` with gold halo radiance.

### Cards
- Constructed with `#0D0D0D` base fill, bounded by `1px solid #242424`.
- Image headers default to a cinematic ratio (16:9 landscape or 2:3 vertical hero poster format) with a bottom gradient fade into `#0D0D0D` (`linear-gradient(180deg, transparent 60%, #0D0D0D 100%)`).
- **Interaction:** On hover, card translates `-4px` vertically, border changes from `#242424` to `#FF3439`, and box-shadow ignites with crimson atmospheric glow (`rgba(230, 36, 41, 0.25)`).

### Chips & Badges
- **Tactical Category Chips:** `#151515` background, `1px solid #2A2A2A`, text styled with `label-badge` in `#D0D0D0`.
- **Status Badges:**
  - *Threat / Live:* `#8B1116` translucent background (`rgba(139, 17, 22, 0.3)`), border `1px solid #E62429`, text `#FF3439`. Includes a pulsing `6px` circular crimson light indicator.
  - *Prestige / Legendary:* `#151515` background, `1px solid #F5C518`, text `#F5C518`.

### Input Fields
- Dark inset containers with `#0D0D0D` background, `1px solid #242424` border, and text set to `#FFFFFF`.
- Placeholder text styled in `#8A8A8A`.
- **Focus State:** Border flashes to Crimson `#E62429` with an inner and outer atmospheric glow ring (`0 0 0 1px #E62429, 0 0 12px rgba(230, 36, 41, 0.3)`).

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- Square base (`4px` radius for checkboxes; full round for radios), `18px x 18px`, framed in `1px solid #2A2A2A` with `#080808` fill.
- Checked state fills with `#E62429` and reveals a razor-sharp `#FFFFFF` checkmark or center pin. Border shifts to `#FF3439` with an immediate micro-glow.

### Lists & Tactical Data Tables
- Row strips separated by subtle `1px solid #1A1A1A` dividers.
- Alternating row zebra tints avoided; instead, individual row hover triggers an instantaneous fill shift to `#151515` along with a `2px` vertical Crimson accent bar positioned on the leading edge.
